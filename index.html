Equipment

Built around the part, not a catalog page

Most benches on the market force your component to fit their plumbing. We start with the component drawing, the acceptance test procedure, and the throughput you need, then design the flow path, instrumentation, and fixturing around it.

  • Component flow benches — valves, nozzles, fuel controls, heat exchangers, filters, actuators.
  • Pressure test stands — proof, burst, cycle, and leak-decay with programmable profiles.
  • Pneumatic & bleed-air rigs — heated air, mass-flow control, and high-temp instrumentation.
  • Flow hardware — orifice plates, metering restrictors, flow dividers, reference standards.
  • Ground support sets — hydraulic mules, N₂ boost carts, portable leak testers.

Process

Four phases, fixed deliverables

  1. Phase 1

    Requirements

    We review the component spec and ATP, define media, ranges, accuracy targets, cycle time, and safety boundaries. Deliverable: a signed requirements matrix and budgetary quote.

  2. Phase 2

    Design

    P&ID, flow-path sizing, relief and burst analysis, instrument selection, panel and fixture CAD, control sequence narrative. Deliverable: design review package with a firm price.

  3. Phase 3

    Build & verify

    Fabrication, machining, wiring, and software, then in-house FAT against your acceptance criteria with real hardware and recorded data. Deliverable: FAT report and calibration certificates.

  4. Phase 4

    Install & sustain

    On-site SAT, operator training, spares kit, and an O&M manual. Deliverable: recurring calibration schedule and support agreement.
